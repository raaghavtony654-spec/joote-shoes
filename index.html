Founded in April 2026 by Adnan Yeolawala, Joote is more than a sneaker brand. It's a bridge between ancient Indian heritage and modern streetwear.

Under Joote Fashion Private Limited, we bring authentic handcrafted techniques from across India directly to your feet. Every pair tells a story of tradition, patience, and meticulous skill.

The Crafts

Kantha

Bengal, East India

A centuries-old embroidery technique using running stitches to create intricate patterns and textures.

Zari

Surat & Varanasi

Fine gold and silver thread woven into rich fabrics, bringing a royal touch to everyday wear.

Kasuti

Karnataka, South India

Complex geometric patterns embroidered without tracing, requiring immense precision.

Chikankari

Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh

Delicate and artful hand embroidery that breathes life into the upper canvas.
